Brief Discussion of the Theory

Organizational Sensemaking Theory

Karl E. Weick's Sensemaking Theory explains how people inside an organization make sense of unclear or changing situations by talking and sharing information with one another. Instead of looking at an organization as just a static chart, the theory focuses on how daily communication helps members agree on what is happening and decide what to do next.

Why Him

Why Karl Weick Represents Our Organization

We chose Karl Weick because our group relies on open discussion whenever we plan tasks, solve problems, or adjust to deadlines. His idea of building shared understanding through communication matches how we coordinate our work as a team.

Importance and Essence of Organizational Communication

Organizational Communication helps us understand how clear messages, active listening, and feedback keep a team or company working well together. It shows that good communication is not only about speaking well, but also about building trust, preventing misunderstandings, and making sure everyone shares the same goals.
